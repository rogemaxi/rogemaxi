## Depth notes (for reviewers)
- **AP Physics C** topics are mapped where the lesson teaches the physics. Calculus-based treatment appears in the Honors sections (2.8, 3.8, 6.9, 10.8) and in the labeled AP Physics C extensions of 15.8 (flux, Gauss’s law, continuous distributions) and 17.9 (Biot–Savart, Ampère’s law, LC circuits); a C-level reviewer must judge depth.
- **IB DP:** HL-only topics (A.4, A.5, B.4, D.4, E.2) are marked in the app. The IB internal-assessment scientific investigation is supported by the labs and Unit 20 but is not provided.
- **NGSS** PEs are three-dimensional. A mapped lesson supports the disciplinary core idea; meeting the PE also requires the named practice.
